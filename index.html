About

Hi, I'm the person behind the needles

Knitting Department is where I share the patterns, tutorials, and yarn tangents that come out of my making practice. I started knitting as a way to make things with my hands — and it turns out, making stuff actually does feel damn good.

Here you'll find free patterns for all skill levels, video walkthroughs on YouTube, and regular updates on Instagram. Everything is designed to be actually usable — no mysterious abbreviations, no vague instructions.

Whether you're picking up needles for the first time or looking for your next project, you're in the right place.
